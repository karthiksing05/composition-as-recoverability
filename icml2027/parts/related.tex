The body covers Frege, Montague, \citet{fodor1988}, \citet{smolensky1990} and the measures nearest to ours. This section adds the work around them.

\textbf{Conceptual combination and behavioral tests.} A guppy is a typical pet fish, yet neither a typical pet nor a typical fish \citep{osherson1981}, and conceptual blending builds wholes with structure of their own \citep{fauconnier2002,janssen1997}. The debate over composition in networks began with connectionist models of language \citep{rumelhart1986,pinker1988,elman1990}, and \citet{marcus1998} argued that networks fail to extend a relation beyond their training space. Meta-learning brings networks to the human level on benchmarks of new combinations \citep{lake2023}, detectors judge generated scenes \citep{ramesh2021}, and synthetic studies follow how diffusion models acquire combinations of attributes \citep{okawa2023} and the rules of a random hierarchy \citep{favero2025diffusion}. All of these tests score outputs and leave the states unscored.

\textbf{Context-dependent constituents.} \citet{smolensky1988} described a looser kind of constituent. Subtracting \emph{cup without coffee} from \emph{cup with coffee} leaves coffee in the context of a cup, and this constituent differs in the context of a can or a tree. Smolensky called the two kinds strong and weak compositional structure \citep{smolensky1987}. His distinction concerns the form a constituent takes, while ours concerns the capabilities a state supports. In the same way, \emph{red} contributes differently to \emph{red hair} and to \emph{red apple} \citep{kamp1995}. \citet{fodor1990} replied to tensor products that implicit constituents cannot act in processing, and the dispute continues \citep{goodale2026}.

\textbf{Procedures that build and decompose.} \citet{vangelder1990} called a representation functionally compositional when general procedures build and decompose it. Reduced descriptions compressed part--whole hierarchies into single patterns \citep{hinton1990}, recursive auto-associative memories learned the procedures \citep{pollack1990}, and vector-symbolic architectures fixed them by design \citep{plate1995,gayler2006,kanerva2009}. Models of analogy bind fillers to roles explicitly \citep{hummel1997}, although binding remains a problem for perception as well as for networks \citep{treisman1980,greff2020}.

\textbf{Reading parts from representations.} Superposition lets a state carry more features than dimensions \citep{elhage2022}. Disentanglement seeks codes that separate the factors of variation \citep{bengio2013,locatello2019}, and identifiability results guarantee generalization for additive decoders \citep{lachapelle2023,wiedemer2024}. \citet{elmoznino2026} score the structure that a whole reveals beyond its parts, a score near zero when the parts' structures already account for the whole.

\textbf{Test-time harnesses.} Recursive harnesses keep a task's context in code and hand the model calls that stay close to its training distribution, and models trained inside such a harness gain on compositional tasks held out from training \citep{zhang2025rlm,zhang2026harness}. Layout planners hand a grounded generator one box per object \citep{lian2024llmgrounded,li2023gligen}. Such systems move the decomposer from the network into code, and we place them by the component that assigns the roles (Section~\ref{sec:harness}).
